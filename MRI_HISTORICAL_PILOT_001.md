# EDGE MRI — Two historical pilot candidates (research gate)

Date: 2026-10-22. Dataset: `investigation/fixtures/mri_historical_candidates_001.json`.

## Case 1: Simplified Chinese Pokémon TCG, mainland China
- **21 September 2022 (candidate cutoff):** Pokémon China announced an online presentation scheduled for 28 September. Contemporaneous item: https://www.pokemon.cn/tcg/product/16380.html
- **28 September 2022 (AFTER cutoff):** Official information confirms a 28 October retail launch and Sun & Moon product line: https://www.pokemon.cn/tcg/other/17201.html
- **27 October 2022 (AFTER cutoff):** Pokémon Shanghai describes local organized-play facilities and plans: https://www.pokemon.cn/tcg/event/16369.html
- **14 March 2025 (outcome context):** The Pokémon Company describes National Championships having reached four first-tier Chinese cities: https://corporate.pokemon.co.jp/en/topics/detail/t-33/

Early candidate hypothesis: localization could seed durable participation beyond speculative collection. Rival: cultural novelty could be transient or concentrated among collectors. Outcome finding: later organized-play activity is consistent with persistence, **not evidence of profitable resale** and not a causal effect of the announcement.

## Case 2: LEGO 10365, Captain Jack Sparrow's Pirate Ship
- **26 August 2025 (candidate cutoff):** LEGO announced release dates, €349.99 RRP and promotional gift-with-purchase: https://www.lego.com/en-us/aboutus/news/2025/august/lego-icons-captain-jack-sparrow-s-pirate-ship
- **26 August 2025 (historical discussion):** Brick Fanatics compares the old 4184 Black Pearl and its advertised aftermarket asking prices; **asking prices are not completed sales**: https://www.brickfanatics.com/lego-10365-jack-sparrow-pirate-ship-revealed
- **12 September 2025 (AFTER cutoff):** Brickset reports LEGO Insiders access, RRP and gift offer: https://brickset.com/article/124805/the-black-pearl-available-now

Early candidate hypothesis: iconic-lore nostalgia and product redesign can redistribute demand across old/new sets. Rival: a new official supply can cannibalize the old edition's premium. Neither can be accepted as a resale forecast without sold-price and liquidity histories.

## Crucial temporal rule
The material was **retrieved in October 2026**. `published_date` is a publisher's date, **not** a proven `observed_at` by EDGE in 2022/2025. The JSON must **not** be injected as if EDGE observed the sources at the historical time, or used to claim a clean ex-ante backtest. A clean prospective replay requires contemporaneous archives or verifiable capture timestamps, with an explicit information cut.
