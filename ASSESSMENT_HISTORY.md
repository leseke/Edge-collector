# Candidate research assessments

This stage evaluates collected evidence with disclosed deterministic rules. It
does not call an LLM, infer actual private motives, predict returns or estimate
probabilities. Research priority is distinct from a financial decision. Hypotheses
are produced even without completed sales; missing sales do not block research.

Each candidate has a stable dossier ID with source-linked publication mentions,
fresh or historical retail asking-price observations, weak signals, actor motive
hypotheses, an early thesis, counter-thesis, conditional scenarios, invalidations,
missing evidence and a concrete next verification. Manufacturer publication,
media coverage and retail quotes retain different evidential meanings. A mention
is not proof of an event, scarcity, popularity or demand. Multiple media sources
are not assumed independent. A failed search is never proof of scarcity.

Titles and collected summaries select announcement/promotion/review contexts.
These remain provisional lexical classifications. Gift-with-purchase context
requires validating region, dates, eligibility and threshold. Retail prices are
asking prices; delivery and net resale margin remain unknown unless collected.
Quotes older than 24 hours remain visible as historical evidence and are excluded
from current-quote coverage. Private incentives remain hypotheses, not facts.

HIGH prioritises official announcement context or a promotion context for research.
MEDIUM prioritises other announcement contexts or current retail observations.
LOW covers remaining mentions requiring context validation. No category is an
investment score or purchase instruction. Existing capital, financial watchlist,
notifications and execution permissions are unaffected.

`assessments.json` and `assessment_report.md` are appended with each existing
snapshot. The semantic fingerprint tracks evidence content, price/stock/freshness,
research action and data gaps. Passing time or another identical capture does not
create a thesis revision; material changes do. First assessment time and previous
fingerprints remain linked through transition records and immutable snapshots.

The readable report summarises every candidate and expands the first three in
priority order. The complete structured file contains every dossier. The current
module supplies an auditable first-pass research assessment; broader sourcing,
human review and genuine market measurements are still needed to test hypotheses.
