    Ciao, Venice: The LEGO Group floats new Icons set

    Download all assets - 252 MB
    • Brand-new LEGO set is a 5,682-piece love letter to Venice and its surrounding neighbourhoods, celebrating the architecture and culture of one of the world's most iconic cities
    • Packed with rich details, from a villa and artisan glassblowing workshop to an astronomical clock tower, gondola and a street piano that plays real notes
    • Includes 17 Minifigures: gondoliers, musicians, tourists and carnival-goers in elaborate masks and costumes
    • Available for LEGO Insiders Early Access from 1 November 2026 from LEGO.com/Venice and LEGO Stores

    BILLUND, DENMARK, 15 OCTOBER 2026: Venice’s iconic canals and centuries-old character are being celebrated today as the LEGO® Icons Venetian Neighbourhood (11388) set is revealed. With 5,682 pieces, this is one of the most intricate city dioramas in the LEGO Icons collection and marks the first time the LEGO Group has created a cityscape-style display model of this scale, inviting builders to capture the romance, rhythm and remarkable architecture of Venice with LEGO elements – no passport required.

    Set along a bustling, brick-built waterfront, this set recreates a canal-side neighbourhood embodying the true spirit of the floating city. A luxury villa, artisan glassblowing workshop and astronomical clock tower line the water’s edge while an arched bridge crosses the canal below, where a gondola can glide through the scene. The diorama can be separated and rearranged into two distinct sections, or the roofs and floors lifted away to discover detailed interiors within each building.

    With its richly layered architecture and display-ready silhouette, the model offers LEGO Icons collectors an ambitious new centrepiece build. The set is designed with playful, hands-on features that capture the enchanting atmosphere of the beloved city. Turn the small flower vase atop the street piano to play real notes or rotate the knob on the side of the clock tower to bring its astronomical calendar into motion.

    The neighbourhood is also home to 17 Minifigures, bringing every corner of the storied scene to life. Gondoliers, musicians, tourists and two carnival-goers dressed in extravagant costumes and elaborate masks make up a delightful tribute to the traditions and festivities that the region is famed for.

    The LEGO Icons Venetian Neighbourhood (11388) set will be available for LEGO Insiders* Early Access from 1 November 2026, and for all from 4 November 2026, priced at £449.99 / €499.99 / $549.99. Discover more and purchase at LEGO.com/Venice or visit your nearest LEGO Store.

    Additionally, shoppers who purchase the set (11388) between 1 – 8 November will receive the LEGO Icons Venetian Water Taxi (40898) as a gift with purchase, available while stocks last**.

    Notes to Editor

    *LEGO Insiders membership required. T&Cs apply.
    **T&Cs apply.

    About the LEGO Group
    The LEGO Group’s mission is to inspire and develop the builders of tomorrow through the power of play. The LEGO System in Play, with its foundation in LEGO bricks, allows children and fans to build and rebuild anything they can imagine.

    The LEGO Group was founded in Billund, Denmark in 1932 by Ole Kirk Kristiansen, its name derived from the two Danish words LEg GOdt, which mean “Play Well”.

    Today, the LEGO Group remains a family-owned company headquartered in Billund. Its products are sold in more than 120 countries worldwide. For more information: www.LEGO.com.
