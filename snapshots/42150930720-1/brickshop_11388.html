Celebrate Italy’s rich cultural and architectural heritage with the LEGO Icons Venetian Neighborhood (11388) travel decor building set – a perfect gift idea for adults and travel lovers.

Unwind as you create a captivating canal-side scene, complete with a luxury villa, an artisan glassblowing workshop and astronomical clock tower. Play notes on the 3D-printed street piano, sail a gondola beneath the arched bridge and bring stories to life with 17 colorful characters, including gondoliers, carnival-goers, tourists and musicians. Remove floors and roofs to reveal detailed interior spaces and easily separate and rearrange sections to explore different display layouts for the home or office. Discover a space for relaxation with the inspiring range of LEGO Icons advanced building sets (sold separately) designed for adults.

Build this city diorama with confidence using the LEGO Builder app, where you can rotate models in 3D, zoom in and track your progress.

  • Celebrate Italian culture with the LEGO® Icons Venetian Neighborhood (11388), a richly detailed diorama inspired by the canals and architecture of the legendary city
  • Craft a highly detailed Venetian waterfront with ornate architecture, an arched bridge, canal and gondola, plus 17 minifigures for storytelling
  • Explore a glassblowing workshop, discover an astronomical clock tower, play notes on the 3D-printed street piano, sail a gondola and meet gondoliers, carnival-goers and musicians
  • Enjoy constructing this outstanding city diorama, arrange the modular sections for the perfect look and display it in your home or office
  • Make a birthday or special occasion extra special with this intricate decor model for fans of European architecture and travel
  • Build with the LEGO Builder app, where you can rotate your model in 3D, zoom in and track your progress
  • Discover a space for relaxation and creativity with the inspiring range of LEGO Icons advanced building sets (sold separately) designed for adults
  • This 5,682-piece diorama kit measures over 18 in. (48 cm) high, 25 in. (63 cm) wide and 12 in. (31 cm) deep
