# EDGE product roadmap

Canonical priorities, ordered by user-visible outcomes. Status is recorded in PROJECT_STATE.md; do not mark complete without evidence.

1. Market Explorer: searchable and filterable real TCG catalog, series, images, product detail, actual active offers and completed sales separately, source and freshness.
2. Market Intelligence: persistent verified historical observations, per-product price and availability history, comparable changes, events, liquidity and explicit missing data.
3. Opportunity Radar: autonomous discovery across TCG, early asymmetry, catalysts, entry price, exit scenarios, risks and evidence classification; no capital-based discovery suppression.
4. EDGE Agent: source-backed investigation and contradiction handling, automated collection and evidence dossiers; clearly distinguish experimental local model from deployed capability.
5. Portfolio and watchlist: tracking, editable capital, commitments, decision history, performance and alerts.
6. Production readiness: responsive cohesive UI, reliability, security, automated tests and operational monitoring. Production deployment only after owner approval.

Deliver vertical slices rather than isolated technical modules. Reorder only with owner approval or document a technical dependency that blocks the next slice.
