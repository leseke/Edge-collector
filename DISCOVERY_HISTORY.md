# Publication-led research candidates

Discovery reads the collected announcements, not a hard-coded product list.
It extracts explicit five-digit LEGO reference candidates from titles and bounded
product contexts in summaries. Historical daily features and statistical roundups
are excluded from launch discovery. These are provisional references, not product
catalogue validation or verified scarcity. No semantic brand-only matches are used.

`discovery.json` preserves stable IDs, first detection, source-linked evidence,
manufacturer/media roles, lexical signal contexts and the last retail research
result. Candidates survive leaving the source feed. ANNOUNCEMENT, REVIEW and
PROMOTION are title-based research contexts, not verified event classifications.
Ranking is a disclosed heuristic, not a profitability score. Capital never filters
discovery or research.

Each run researches at most three candidates on LDLC: two by oldest research time
then signal/source score and recency, plus a recent review as a current-market
control. Rotation prevents the same unavailable future sets monopolising requests.
An exact-reference search can redirect to a product or expose product links; at
most two links are checked. Recommendations on a search page are never accepted
without the existing exact-reference/EUR/seller checks. Search failures, no verified
offer in the bounded sample, and verified retail offers remain distinct outcomes.
None proves exhaustive market coverage or availability in France after delivery.

Verified offers can enter a separate research monitoring list (maximum four extra
targets) for recurring prices and declared stock. The manual financial watchlist,
capital, notifications, buy signals and purchase permissions are untouched.
Validated product/seller identity is not a valuation or investment recommendation.
The capacity cap controls request count, not financing or candidate retention.

Current discoveries feed the explicit SKU links used by the reaction report.
Reactions still require actual pre-event and post-event observations. Discovery
does not reconstruct observations that were never collected. The same six-hour
workflow archives discovery/search evidence, the derived monitoring list and an
updated full SHA-256 manifest.
